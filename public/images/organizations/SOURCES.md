# Organization marks

Downloaded 2026-11-07 for identification of academic affiliations and employers. Logos remain the property of their respective organizations.

- `university-of-tehran.png`: https://nlp-ut.github.io/ — extracted unchanged from the base64 PNG used as the “UT Logo” in the official University of Tehran NLP laboratory website bundle: https://nlp-ut.github.io/static/js/main.b7843106.js (152 × 152).
- `university-of-isfahan.png`: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:University_of_Isfahan_Logo.png — AliGoli2006, CC0 1.0. Original image: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/42/University_of_Isfahan_Logo.png (160 × 159). Emblem identity cross-checked against the university website.
- `university-of-isfahan-wordmark.png`: https://ui.ac.ir/uploads/90/2025/Oct/18/Frame%203-min-min.png — official university website header (212 × 85).
- `intrest.svg`: https://intrest.ca/landing/intrest-brand-logo.svg — official website logo (57 × 56 viewBox).
- `adan-ai.png`: extracted unchanged from the embedded PNG in https://adanai.ir/assets/adan-logo-DCGBbiH6.svg — official Adan AI header logo (355 × 293). https://www.linkedin.com/company/adan-ai identifies adanai.ir as the Tehran company website.

The two PNG extraction steps decode existing image resources; they do not redraw or recolor the marks.

- `acm.svg`: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/develop/icons/acm.svg — Simple Icons (CC0) ACM diamond vector; brand source https://identitystandards.acm.org/. Uses ACM Blue (#0085CA); geometry unchanged.
- `tedx.png`: https://landing-pages.ted.com/tedx-logo-generator/assets/logo.png — official TEDx Logo Generator header mark (1900 × 555, transparent). Used to identify TEDx alongside the full TEDxUniversityOfIsfahan event name. The mark includes the x and is not the TED-only logo.
